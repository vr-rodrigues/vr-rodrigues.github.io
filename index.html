Victor Rangel

Economist working on causal inference, public policy, political economy, and large language models.

I have a Bachelor's degree in Economics and an M.A. in Public Policy from Insper. I have professional and academic experience in data analysis, focusing on applied econometrics and machine learning.

I spend most of my days running acceptably biased OLS models. I also communicate science and teach econometrics through @econometriafacil (200,000+ followers), and write the Econometria Fácil column at Folha de S.Paulo.
